Project Overview

This project analyzes retail transaction data from a UK-based online retailer. I used PostgreSQL to prepare the dataset, calculate sales metrics, and identify patterns in products, customers, and geographic markets.

SQL Code Walkthrough

Explore the actual queries used to prepare and analyze the dataset. Click a section to view the SQL code, output, and explanation.

Business Insights

November 2011 generated the highest monthly positive sales. The United Kingdom was the largest market, contributing approximately 84.6% of positive sales.

Product rankings also showed why shipping and adjustment entries should be distinguished from merchandise.

Project Limitations

The analysis focuses on positive transactions, not net revenue or profit. December 2011 is incomplete, and some transactions have missing customer IDs. Product classification remains preliminary.

Dataset: UCI Online Retail
